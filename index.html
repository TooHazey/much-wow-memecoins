The lore

A very abridged timeline of jokes that got out of hand.

  1. 2013

    Dogecoin

    Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer fork Litecoin tech and slap a Shiba Inu on it as a parody of crypto hype. It becomes the original meme coin — and a top-10 asset years later.

  2. 2020

    Shiba Inu

    An anonymous founder, "Ryoshi," launches the self-styled "Dogecoin killer." Half the supply is sent to Vitalik Buterin, who later burns most of it and donates the rest.

  3. 2021

    The Elon era

    Tweets move markets. DOGE runs from fractions of a cent to roughly $0.70 in May 2021 amid a celebrity- and retail-fueled frenzy.

  4. 2022

    BONK

    Airdropped to the Solana community on Christmas, BONK kickstarts Solana's meme season after the FTX collapse.

  5. 2023

    PEPE & WIF

    A frog and a dog in a knitted hat. PEPE rockets on Ethereum in spring; dogwifhat becomes Solana's mascot by year end.

  6. 2024

    Launchpad mania

    Bonding-curve launchpads like pump.fun make spinning up a token take seconds. Millions of coins launch; almost all go to zero.

  7. 2025

    Political memes

    $TRUMP launches days before the U.S. inauguration and briefly hits a multibillion-dollar valuation, pushing meme coins into mainstream politics.

Degen field guide

Six red flags to check before you ape in.

01

Unlocked liquidity

If the liquidity pool isn't locked or burned, the dev can pull it and leave you holding a token nobody can buy.

02

Whale concentration

A handful of wallets holding a big slice of supply means one sell can crater the chart. Check the holder list.

03

Honeypot contracts

Some contracts let you buy but block selling. Test with a tiny amount or use a honeypot checker first.

04

Mint & freeze authority

On Solana, active mint authority means infinite new supply; freeze authority means your tokens can be locked.

05

Paid hype

Sudden influencer shills and bot-filled chats often signal a coordinated pump — and a coordinated dump.

06

FOMO entries

Buying after a 50x candle is how exit liquidity is made. Only risk what you'd be fine losing entirely.
