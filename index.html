Where memes live

Meme sub-sectors by chain and theme, by market cap.

Solana

The home of fast, cheap launches. BONK, WIF and the pump.fun launchpad made Solana the busiest meme chain.

Ethereum

Home of the older memes. SHIB, PEPE and FLOKI started here. Fees are higher, and so are the market caps.

BNB & Base

Exchange-backed chains with their own launchpads (Four.meme on BNB, Zora-style coins on Base) and big retail user bases.

Everywhere else

TON, Sui, Tron and even Bitcoin have meme scenes. Wherever block space is cheap, a dog coin shows up eventually.

The lore

A timeline of internet jokes that got out of hand.

  1. Dec 2013

    Dogecoin

    Billy Markus and Jackson Palmer fork Litecoin-era tech and put a Shiba Inu on it as a parody of crypto hype. It becomes the original meme coin.

  2. 2014

    To NASCAR

    The Dogecoin community crowdfunds a sponsorship for driver Josh Wise's car, an early sign of what a meme community can pay for.

  3. Aug 2020

    Shiba Inu

    An anonymous founder, "Ryoshi," launches the self-styled "Dogecoin killer." Half the supply goes to Vitalik Buterin, who later burns most of it and donates the rest.

  4. May 2021

    The Elon era

    Tweets move markets. DOGE climbs from under a cent to about $0.70 during a celebrity- and retail-driven frenzy.

  5. Nov 2021

    Squid Game rug

    A token named after the hit show pumps thousands of percent, then its developers disappear with the money. It became the standard example of a rug pull.

  6. Dec 2022

    BONK

    Airdropped to the Solana community at Christmas, BONK kicks off Solana's meme season after the FTX collapse.

  7. 2023

    PEPE & WIF

    A frog and a dog in a knitted hat. PEPE takes off on Ethereum in spring, and dogwifhat becomes Solana's mascot by the end of the year.

  8. Jan 2024

    pump.fun

    Bonding-curve launchpads make it possible to create a token in seconds. Millions launch, and almost all of them go to zero.

  9. Mar 2024

    Hat on the Sphere

    WIF holders crowdfund to put the hat dog on the Las Vegas Sphere, showing how far a meme community will go for a bit.

  10. Jan 2025

    $TRUMP

    Launched days before the U.S. inauguration, it briefly reaches a multibillion-dollar valuation and brings meme coins into mainstream politics.

  11. Feb 2025

    LIBRA

    A token promoted by Argentina's president collapses within hours of launch and sets off a political scandal. Celebrity endorsement doesn't make a coin safe.

  12. Today

    Your move

    Thousands of new tokens launch every day. Most last less than a week. The live board above shows the ones still standing.

01

Unlocked liquidity

If the liquidity pool isn't locked or burned, the dev can pull it and leave you holding a token nobody can buy.

02

Whale concentration

If a handful of wallets hold a big share of supply, one sale can crash the chart. Check the holder list.

03

Honeypot contracts

Some contracts let you buy but block selling. Test with a tiny amount or use a honeypot checker first.

04

Mint & freeze authority

On Solana, active mint authority means new supply can be created without limit, and freeze authority means your tokens can be locked.

05

Paid hype

Sudden influencer promotion and bot-filled chats often signal a coordinated pump, followed by a coordinated dump.

06

FOMO entries

People who buy after a 50x candle are usually the ones early holders sell to. Only risk what you'd be fine losing completely.

    Honest FAQ

    What actually makes a meme coin worth anything?

    Only what the next buyer will pay. There are usually no cash flows, no product and no claim on anything. The value comes from attention, community and liquidity. That's why prices can rise 100x and fall 99% so quickly.

    What is a bonding curve launch?

    Launchpads like pump.fun sell new tokens along a price curve, so each purchase raises the price for the next buyer. When the curve fills, the liquidity moves to a regular decentralized exchange. Launching costs almost nothing, so there are a huge number of tokens.

    Why do most meme coins go to zero?

    Attention runs out. Early holders and insiders sell into new demand, and once new buyers stop arriving, nothing holds the price up. Studies of launchpads keep finding that only a tiny share of tokens ever reach a meaningful market cap.

    Is this investment advice?

    No. This site is for entertainment and education. Meme coins are among the most volatile assets in existence. Never invest money you can't afford to lose, and be wary of anyone promising returns.
